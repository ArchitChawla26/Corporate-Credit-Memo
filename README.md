# Corporate Credit Memo — CVS Health Corporation (NYSE: CVS)

An institutional-style credit committee memo on CVS Health, built end to end from the company's SEC filings: issuer and business profile, capital structure, five years of ratio analysis plus an LTM view, liquidity and maturity profile, a shadow rating assessment, and an approve-with-conditions recommendation.

**Shadow rating: BBB, stable outlook. Recommendation: APPROVE senior unsecured exposure at investment-grade terms, with monitoring conditions (see memo, Section 8).**

*Independent research project by Archit Chawla. Not investment advice and not a credit rating.*

Adjusted EBITDA adds back only charges that are separate line items in the consolidated statements of operations or discrete items in the filings' segment reconciliations (goodwill impairment; opioid and legacy litigation charges; loss on assets held for sale; restructuring). Insurance premium deficiency reserves are **not** added back. The memo also shows reported-EBITDA ratios (FY2025 gross leverage 6.97x) so the unadjusted picture is visible.

## Method in brief

- **Source:** SEC EDGAR XBRL company facts. Annual figures are the latest-filed 10-K values for FY2021–FY2025; interim figures from the Q2 2026 and Q2 2025 10-Qs. LTM = FY2025 − H1 2025 + H1 2026; balance-sheet items at period end.
- **Debt:** long-term debt (noncurrent) + current portion + short-term borrowings at carrying value. Ties to the FY2025 10-K borrowings note ($64,570M at Dec 31, 2025); the maturity ladder from the same note sums to the disclosed $63,709M gross principal.
- **FFO:** approximation — net income + depreciation & amortization + goodwill impairment. It excludes deferred taxes, stock compensation and other non-cash items a full agency FFO build would include.
- **Verification:** the workbook was recalculated in LibreOffice after building; all 132 ratio cells matched an independent Python recomputation from `data/inputs.json`. Key balance-sheet figures were also tied by hand to the FY2025 10-K and Q2 2026 10-Q.
- **Agency ratings** (Moody's Baa3 stable / S&P BBB stable / Fitch BBB negative) were verified from a public ratings database (InvestmentGrade.com) and are cited for reference; the BBB shadow rating is my own assessment.


## Limitations

Period-end leverage only (intra-period commercial paper swings are not captured); adjusted EBITDA involves judgment about which charges are non-recurring, and "one-off" litigation charges have recurred in some form in three of the last five years; insurance reserve adequacy is taken as reported; H1 2026 figures are unaudited; June 2026 finance lease liabilities are carried at December 2025 values because the Q2 2026 condensed balance sheet does not break them out. Full discussion in the memo, Section 10.
